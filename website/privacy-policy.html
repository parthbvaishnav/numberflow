Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how NP Game Studio handles information, analytics, and data security across our Android applications, including NP AI Video and Number Flow.

Last Updated: April 2026 NP AI Video Number Flow
1

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number) directly from users within NP AI Video or Number Flow.

However, our applications may utilize trusted third-party services that automatically collect technical non-personal information to ensure stable app performance, show relevant ads, and improve gameplay experience, including:

  • Device hardware information (device model, operating system version, screen resolution)
  • App usage data (game progress, focus metrics, feature usage frequency, and session duration)
  • Advertising ID (used for serving customized and non-intrusive advertisements)
  • System performance data (crash logs, error diagnostics, and network latency)
2

Third-Party Services

To provide high-quality mobile experiences on Android, NP Game Studio integrates reliable third-party SDKs:

Google AdMob

Used for displaying advertisements within our apps. Google AdMob may use device advertising identifiers to serve relevant ads.

AppLovin

We work with AppLovin to deliver ads in our mobile application and other devices and/or platforms. For more information about AppLovin’s collection and use of your information visit: https://legal.applovin.com/privacy/.

Firebase Analytics

Used for analyzing app performance, user interactions, feature usage, and diagnosing crash reports to improve application stability.

These third-party service providers collect and process data in accordance with their respective privacy policies and our in-app Consent Management & Age Gating choices.

3

How We Use Information

The technical information collected is used exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To enhance and personalize video creation workflows and number puzzle gameplay
  • To diagnose technical errors, fix bugs, and optimize app loading speed on Android
  • To display non-intrusive and appropriate advertisements
  • To evaluate overall app usage trends and plan future feature updates
4

Data Security

NP Game Studio values your trust. We implement standard industry security practices to safeguard data processed by our apps. However, please note that no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

5

Children’s Privacy

Our applications feature an in-app Age Gate & Consent Management system. We strictly comply with COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) and AppLovin / Google AdMob policies:

  • Users under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable European jurisdictions) are automatically classified as age-restricted users.
  • We do NOT initialize or serve personalized advertising or track identifiers for users identified as children.
  • No personal information is collected from or requested from child users.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has inadvertently provided personal information, please contact us immediately at npgamestudio1@gmail.com so we can take immediate corrective measures.

6

Our website and apps may contain links to external web pages (such as Google Play Store listings or third-party ad links). NP Game Studio is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or data collection policies of external third-party websites.

7

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, legal requirements, or service improvements. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

8

Meta Audience Network & Third-Party Ad Services

Our applications integrate third-party advertising services including Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, and AppLovin MAX to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements. These advertising networks may collect anonymous device identifiers, IP addresses, and app interaction data to optimize ad delivery, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance in compliance with applicable privacy laws.

For more information on how Meta processes your data, please review the Meta Privacy Policy and Meta Platform Terms.

User Data Deletion Instructions

Under Meta Platform Terms and global privacy regulations, users have the right to request deletion of any personal data or advertising identifiers associated with their use of our applications.

To request complete data deletion, please email npgamestudio1@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - Number Flow" along with your Device Advertising ID or User ID. All associated data will be deleted within 30 days.
